NES Super Mario Bros. sprites, cut from the sheets on The Spriters Resource
(Mario & Luigi, Enemies & Bosses, Items/Objects, Item and Brick Blocks, World 1-1 map)
and scaled up 3x with nearest-neighbour so they stay crisp on the Scratch stage.
The backdrops are a 160x120 window of the World 1-1 map with the sky recoloured.

The characters and artwork are Nintendo's. They're used here for a free, non-commercial
fan demo, the same way thousands of Scratch projects use them.
